  # About soren.codes

## Software engineer & digital explorer

Hi, I'm **Sören Maschmann** — the person behind **soren.codes**. I'm a software engineer and digital explorer based in Bielefeld, Germany, and I've specialized in full-stack web technologies since graduating in media informatics.

Transforming visions into reality is what drives me: taking an idea, shaping it together with the people who need it, and turning it into a working product — one line of code at a time. I believe that every vision can be implemented as long as we keep exchanging ideas and priorities openly. That shared understanding is the real basis for every successful project.

## What I do

I design and build individual software: websites, web apps, and digital products that are tailored to your vision rather than squeezed into a template. My work combines innovation, precision, and passion — from the first sketch of an architecture to the last detail of the user interface. Media informatics gave me the foundation; countless projects since then have sharpened my instincts for what makes software usable, maintainable, and fun.

When I'm not coding, you'll find me exploring the digital frontier — tinkering with open source, testing new frameworks, and sharing what I learn along the way.

## Let's work together

If you have a vision that wants to become reality, I'd love to hear about it. Reach out via the [contact form](/#contact), write me at [work@soren.codes](mailto:work@soren.codes), or find me on [GitHub](https://github.com/dontmash) and [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/smaschmann). Code the future — innovate, together!
